How this works

  • Prices are copied from each provider's own public price page and show the date they were checked. Providers without a public price are marked "Quote only".
  • Rates differ by user type. Pick "Academic lab" or "Industry" above. Rates for a facility's own staff are not shown, since they don't apply to outside users.
  • Like for like: cards marked "partial scope" cover only part of the work (for example library prep without sequencing). They are never marked "lowest", and "Full service only" hides them.
  • Project planner: add several services and see the cheapest total, from one provider or by mixing, using only full-service prices.
  • Check your quotes: when you log a quote, it is compared per unit with the provider's public price or the community median, and flagged if it looks high or suspiciously low.
  • Turnaround is shown only where the provider's page states it, and "Fastest" sorts by it.
  • Volume tips: when ordering a few more samples unlocks a bulk tier that costs less in total, the card says so.
  • Cost curve: open it under any service to see total cost against sample count and where each provider's bulk price kicks in.
  • Community quotes: if you log a quote you received and share it anonymously, others see the median for that provider.

For providers

  • Listing is free. Every price here was copied from a public page, with a link to the source. Nobody has paid to be listed or ranked.
  • Ranking is by price only for the user type and sample count the visitor chose. If paid placement ever exists, it will be labelled and will not change the price order.
  • Correct or complete your listing: send the current price page or list and we will update it. Offers that cover only part of a service (for example library prep) are marked as partial scope, so tell us if yours includes more.
