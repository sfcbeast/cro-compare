    How this works

    Prices come from provider pages

    Each price links to its source and shows when it was checked. Providers without a public price are marked "Quote only".

    Rates depend on who you are

    Choose Academic or Industry. A facility's own staff rates are not shown, because they do not apply to outside users.

    Like for like

    Offers marked "partial scope" cover only part of the work, such as library prep without sequencing. They are never called the cheapest, and "Full service only" hides them.

    Volume tips and cost curves

    When ordering a few more samples unlocks a cheaper bulk tier, the card says so. The cost curve shows where each provider's bulk price starts.

    Compare side by side

    Tick "Compare" on up to four offers of one service to see prices, tiers, turnaround and scope in one table.

    Quotes you receive

    Log a quote and it is checked per unit against the public price or the community median. Sharing a quote anonymously helps other labs.

    For providers

    Listing is free

    Every price here was copied from a public page, with a link to the source. Nobody has paid to be listed or ranked.

    Ranked by price only

    Ranking follows the visitor's user type and sample count. If paid placement ever exists, it will be labelled and will not change the price order.

    Correct or complete your listing

    Send your current price page and it will be updated. Tell us if your offer covers more than the partial scope shown.
