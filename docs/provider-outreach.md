# Provider outreach drafts

Nothing here has been sent. Edit, then send from your own email.

Companion file: `listings-to-confirm.csv` has every listing grouped by organisation (price as published, check date, source link). Paste the rows for one organisation into the email so they can confirm quickly.

## Order of work

1. **Confirm accuracy first (all providers).** Costs the provider nothing and makes listings more trustworthy. Many will correct a price or tell you what is included.
2. **Only then, a pilot with 3 to 5 commercial providers.** Ask for nothing until you can show real requests.

## Template A: confirm your listing (cores and commercial)

Subject: Quick check: is this price still correct? (free listing)

>

>
> [paste rows from listings-to-confirm.csv]
>
> Could you tell me if any of these are out of date, or if the price includes more than I noted (for example analysis, repeats, or sequencing as well as library prep)? Corrections go live as soon as I receive them. The listing is free, and rankings are by price only, so nothing here is paid placement.
>
> Thanks,
> [your name]
> https://sfcbeast.github.io/cro-compare/

## Template B: pilot with a commercial provider (send only after Template A)

Subject: Small labs asking for quotes on [service]: pilot?

> Hi [name],
>

>
> I would like to run a free pilot: for [3] months, requests that match your service go to you with a complete scope. At the end I will share how many were sent and how many you replied to, and we can decide if a small per-qualified-request fee would make sense. No payment is asked now.
>
> If this is not the right contact, could you point me to the person who handles new customer enquiries?
>
> [your name]

## Success measures for the pilot

- At least 3 providers reply to Template A (accuracy), and each correction is applied on the site.
- At least 2 commercial providers agree to a pilot.
- After the pilot, a provider can say how many requests it received and whether it won any order. That is the number to base a lead fee on. Do not set a price before you have it.

## Things to avoid

- Do not claim traffic or volume you do not have yet.
- Do not offer ranking or placement for payment before a provider asks; keep the "ranking by price only" promise.
- Do not scrape contact details; use the contact page or sales address on each provider's own website.
